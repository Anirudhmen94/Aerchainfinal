ABC Snacks Manufacturing Pvt Ltd (headquartered in Pune) is a leading packaged snacks producer with primary operations at our Chakan facility. We manufacture and distribute chips, namkeen, and premium snack products across India and select export markets.

For FY2024–25 we need quotations for approximately 30 SKUs of corrugated packaging to replace and expand our supplier base:

1) Outer shipper cartons — RSC and regular slotted designs; mostly 3-ply for domestic distribution and select 5-ply for export / heavier products.
2) Die-cut display trays and promotional cartons for retail visibility.
3) 7-ply master cartons for palletised export shipments.

Historical annual spend for corrugated packaging is about Rs 3.8 crore; volumes for the coming year are expected to be similar or slightly higher.

Requirements:
- Food-contact safe; protect snacks through the supply chain; compliant with current food safety and export standards.
- Print: unprinted blanks to 1–2 colour flexographic printing with brand marks and regulatory information.
- Deliveries weekly to Chakan plant (Pune district), starting within 8–10 weeks of order placement.
- Pricing: delivered to facility, exclusive of GST. Trial orders before annual contracts.

Vendor must-haves (quality knockouts):
- Current ISO 9001 certification.
- Burst Strength (BCT) test reports for all quoted cartons.
- In-house ECT/burst testing capability (mandatory).
- FSC certification or recycled-content declaration preferred.
- Confirm capacity for weekly delivery schedule.
- References from established food or snacks manufacturers.

Please quote within 14 days of receiving this RFQ, clearly referencing line item numbers with itemised pricing for each SKU.
