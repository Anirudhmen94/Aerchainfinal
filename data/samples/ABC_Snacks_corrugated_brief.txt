ABC Snacks Manufacturing Pvt Ltd — Corrugated Packaging RFQ Brief
Plant: Chakan (Pune) · Category: Corrugated shippers, trays & master cartons · FY26

We are a packaged snacks manufacturer (chips, namkeen, extruded snacks) with primary operations at our Chakan facility near Pune. For the next financial year we need quotations for approximately 28–30 SKUs of corrugated packaging to refresh and expand our supplier base. Historical annual spend is about Rs 3.8 crore; volumes for the coming year are expected to be similar or slightly higher.

SCOPE OF SUPPLY (build ~28–30 line items covering these families)

2) Die-cut display / shelf trays for retail visibility (chips and namkeen).
3) 7-ply master / export cartons for palletised shipments.
4) Partition inserts and a few corrugated sheets / pads as needed for fragile SKUs.
